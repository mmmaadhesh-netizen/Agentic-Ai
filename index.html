What is the core difference between BFS and DFS in graph traversal, and when is BFS optimal?

Click to flip & reveal answer ↷
SYNTHESIS & EXPLANATION

BFS (Breadth-First Search) explores neighbor-by-neighbor using a FIFO Queue. It is optimal for finding the shortest path on unweighted graphs. DFS explores depth using a LIFO Stack and is suited for connectivity and topological sorting.

💡 Agent Mnemonic: "BFS = Broad Flow in Streets (Shortest distance); DFS = Deep Fall into Secrets."
